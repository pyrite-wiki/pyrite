- A software KB's `board.yaml` that cannot be loaded (it does not parse, is not a mapping, has a lane with no `statuses`, a field of the wrong type, a gate policy other than `warn` or `enforce`, or a criterion naming a checker that does not exist) is now refused, instead of the gate or WIP limit it declares being skipped. `sw_claim`, `sw_transition`, `sw_review`, `sw_pull_next`, `sw_check_ready`, `sw_refine`, `sw_board`, `sw_review_queue` and the `pyrite sw` commands that call them answer `VALIDATION_FAILED` (not retryable) with a message naming `board.yaml` and the problem, and claim, move or report nothing; `kb_orient` reports it as `board_error` in its software block. A file over the YAML limits, unreadable, or outside the KB keeps its own error code. With no `board.yaml`, or a valid one, nothing changes. The board config is read from the KB's own root: a `sw` tool called for a KB that does not resolve is now refused (`KB_NOT_FOUND`), `pyrite sw pull-next -k <kb>` now applies that KB's WIP limit, `pyrite sw refine` with no KB is refused, and `sw_board`, `sw_review_queue` and `sw_pull_next` called with no KB use the built-in board (#1538).
