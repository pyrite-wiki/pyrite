A refused `importance` (a scalar the rule refuses: 7.5, 0, 11, "high", NaN) is no longer shown silently. `qa validate`, for the whole KB and for one entry (`--entry`, REST, MCP), reports it as `importance_range` ("a whole number 1-10", no "rename the key" advice) from the file's own value; a mapping or list under `importance` keeps the rename advice. `get` and `search` rows carry an `entry_warnings` list on that entry (CLI, REST and MCP, whole and under `fields`), and CSV output writes a note per entry to stderr. Readers still show the legacy whole-number reading; a missing or accepted `importance` (5.0, "05") says nothing (#1761).
