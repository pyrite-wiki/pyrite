- A `kb.yaml` whose `types` block, a type definition, or a type's `fields` or `endpoints` block is a scalar or a list is now refused with `KB_CONFIG_INVALID` on every surface, instead of a traceback or a silently dropped type. A null block reads as absent: `types: null` declares no types, `types: {memo: }` declares `memo` with defaults, and a bare `fields:` or `endpoints:` declares none (#1591).
