- `pyrite create` and `pyrite update` given more than one body source (`--body`, `--body-file`, `--stdin`) now refuse with a usage error (exit 2) naming the sources given, before anything is written. They used to silently write one of them (stdin, then the file, then `--body`) and drop the rest. `task create` and `task update` already refused; the entry commands now share their check. One source behaves exactly as before (#1780).
