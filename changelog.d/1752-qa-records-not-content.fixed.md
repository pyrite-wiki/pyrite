`qa assess` no longer changes what `qa validate`, `qa status`, `qa gaps` and `export site` report: the `qa_assessment` records it writes are no longer counted as content, flagged as orphans, or exported as a `qa_assessment` folder in the site. `qa validate` `total` and `checked` now count content entries only, and `qa validate` no longer checks the `qa_assessment` records themselves (title, body, date, importance).
