`links discover`, `links batch-suggest` and the `kb_discover_neighbors` / `kb_batch_suggest` tools (and their REST routes) now leave out entries that have no body and no summary (blank after trimming whitespace), so title-only stubs no longer fill the suggestions (#200). The exclusion is applied inside the search query, so `limit` results still come back when enough entries qualify. Pass `--include-empty` (CLI) or `include_empty` (MCP, REST) to get them back. `kb_search` is unchanged: search hides nothing.
