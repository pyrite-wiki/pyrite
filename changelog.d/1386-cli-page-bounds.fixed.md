- **`pyrite list-entries` and `pyrite recent` refuse a negative `--limit` or `--offset` (#1386).** A negative bound was passed to SQLite, where `LIMIT -1` means "no limit", so the whole KB came back and `list-entries` reported `has_more: true` although nothing was left. Both commands now refuse a negative page bound with a usage error (`USAGE_ERROR`, exit 2), as `orient --offset` already did.
