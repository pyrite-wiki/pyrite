- In a bulk create, import, or dry run, paths taken by earlier items in the batch are compared using `entry_paths.fold` on every disk (case-insensitively and canonically normalised). On a case-sensitive disk (Linux), two entries in the same batch whose paths differ only in letter case (`Abc` and `abc`) now step the second entry to `-2` (or refuse a colliding `file_pattern` name) rather than writing two files that would collide when cloned on a case-insensitive disk (macOS or Windows). Dry run and real write agree on all platforms. (#1623)
