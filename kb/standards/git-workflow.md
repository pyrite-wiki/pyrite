## Commit Messages
Follow conventional commits: feat, fix, docs, test, refactor, chore.
Keep subject line concise, use body for details.

For current branch, hook, and pull-request steps, follow
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#branches-hooks-and-pull-requests).
